Terms of use

Personal, authorized use

The Database Backups is intended for the account owner's own private backup and recovery needs. Authorize only Google accounts and application data you own or have permission to manage. These public information pages do not grant access to the private application or its archives.

Owner responsibilities

The owner manages account permissions, backup retention, recovery keys and restoration choices. Keep the recovery key separately from the encrypted archive. Check completed backup status and verify restoration procedures before relying on a backup.

Availability and restoration

Backups depend on the private server, internet access, Google Drive authorization, available storage and provider quotas. Operations can be delayed or fail. Restoration may require provider sign-in, server configuration and validation. The utility does not guarantee continuous availability, a particular recovery time, or successful restoration in every circumstance.

Provider terms and privacy

Google Drive and GitHub Pages remain subject to their respective provider terms. Data handling is described in the privacy policy. This site and utility are not Google products or endorsements.

Support

Contact the operator using the support email shown on the OAuth consent screen. Do not post credentials, private application data, archives or recovery keys publicly.
